What We Do

OUR SERVICES

We offer complete performance solutions, from Stage tunes to professional dynamometer calibration.

ECU Tuning

Professional ECU remapping to safely maximize horsepower and torque output.

Stage Tuning

Stage-based calibrations (Stage 1, 2, 3) tailored to your vehicle's modification level.

Pops & Bangs

ECU-based exhaust tuning for that aggressive crackle and pop sound on throttle lift.

Turbo Tuning

Turbo kit calibration, turbo upgrades and real-time boost monitoring on the dyno.

E85 Tuning

Dedicated E85 ethanol calibration - more power and better response taking full advantage of the fuel.

LS Swap Tuning

Specialists in LS swap calibration with deleted sensors, modified voltages and custom configurations.

Transmission Tuning

Shift point adjustment, line pressure, torque management and automatic-to-manual conversion.

Dyno Rental

Bring your own tuner or simply measure your vehicle's horsepower. Hourly rental with full access to our Dyno Dynamics.

Street Tuning

Affordable street-based calibration option for those looking for performance without a dyno session.

ECU Unlock

ECU unlocking for tuning: GPEC2 / GPEC2A, E41, GM E90/T93 and more.

Standalone ECU

Standalone system tuning: MoTeC, Haltech, FuelTech, AEM, Link ECU, MegaSquirt, Hondata and more.

Deletes

Software-based removal: DPF, EGR, SCR, O2, CAT, IMMO OFF and emissions code correction.

Vehicle Coding

Activation of hidden factory features, module customization and advanced vehicle configuration.

Modification Consulting
